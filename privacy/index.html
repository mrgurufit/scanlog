Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

This explains what data is collected when you visit mrgurufit.com or use the Scan & Log app, how it is used, and with whom it is shared. Data controller: Ido Gura (Mr Guru Fit), Israel — mr.guru.fit@gmail.com.

In short: we do not sell data, show ads, or use third-party tracking networks. The only thing collected about you on the site is an email address — and only if you type it in — plus a fully anonymous visit count. In the app, data lives on your device, and only if you choose to sign in is it backed up to the cloud under your own account.

What the website collects

  • Email address — only if you submit the signup form. Stored by MailerLite (email provider) and used to send the daily episode and updates. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.
  • Anonymous visit count — the site counts one visit per browsing session, with no cookies and no identifying data (no IP, no name, no cross-site tracking), via Make.com. You can opt out — see "Choice & control".
  • Local browser storage — your language, display preferences and a "seen this session" flag are kept in your browser only and never sent anywhere.

What the app collects

Your food log, weight and health data stay on your device. If you sign in with Google, data is backed up to the cloud (Google Firebase) under your account only. Full detail in the app policy: Scan & Log Privacy Policy.

Third parties (service providers)

Each processes data under its own policy: MailerLite (email), Make.com (visit counting & message processing), Google / Firebase (app sign-in & cloud backup), Open Food Facts (barcode lookup — only the barcode is sent), Cloudinary (uploaded product photos), MyMemory (product-name translation), GitHub Pages (hosting), and Google Play / Google Groups (beta distribution). We never sell or rent personal data.

Your rights & choice / control

  • Access, correction, deletion: under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law (and, for EU visitors, the GDPR) you may access, correct or request deletion of your data. Email mr.guru.fit@gmail.com; handled within 30 days.
  • Unsubscribe: one click at the bottom of any email.
  • Opt out of visit counting: choose "No analytics" in the first-visit notice, or browse in private mode. Your choice is stored in your browser.
  • App data: uninstall to delete local data; sign out in Settings to stop cloud sync; email us to delete cloud data.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security & retention

Data is protected by reasonable measures, though no internet transmission is 100% secure. We keep each item only as long as needed for its purpose or until you ask us to delete it.

Not medical advice

The content and app are general information only and are not medical or nutritional advice. Consult a professional before changing how you eat or train.
